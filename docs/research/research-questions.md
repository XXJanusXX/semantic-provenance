# Research Questions

The public research programme is organised around three research questions aligned with its three objectives.

## RQ1 — Semantic preservation

**To what extent can structured semantic-preservation measures detect and quantify the preservation or loss of task-relevant semantic information across AI-driven transformations?**

RQ1 examines whether explicit semantic structures and invariant-level evaluation can identify preservation and loss involving entities, relations, constraints, negation, temporal information, quantification, modality, policy, events, quantities and units.

The question concerns measurable performance under specified conditions. It does not presume that all semantic content can be represented or detected.

## RQ2 — Cryptographic provenance and integrity

**To what extent can canonicalization, content addressing, digital signatures and provenance verification provide reproducible evidence of integrity and traceability across semantic transformation workflows?**

RQ2 examines deterministic evidence representation and verification across transformation stages. It distinguishes content integrity and provenance traceability from the correctness or fidelity of the semantic content itself.

Digital-signature authenticity is within the evaluated scope only when end-to-end signing, key identity binding and verification are implemented and tested.

## RQ3 — Reproducible empirical evaluation

**How can reproducible datasets, benchmark protocols and experimental procedures be designed to evaluate the effectiveness, reliability and limitations of semantic-preservation and provenance mechanisms?**

RQ3 concerns the methodological infrastructure required to support or reject the research hypotheses. It includes benchmark construction, independent ground truth, experimental controls, frozen configurations, statistical procedures, execution records and reproducibility evidence.

RQ3 is methodological. It does not require a separate confirmatory statistical hypothesis.

## Relationship to supporting investigations

Interoperability, distributed-ledger anchoring, multilingual representation and enterprise deployment may be investigated as supporting or exploratory work where evidence and resources permit. They are not independent confirmatory research questions in this three-question public framework unless the research protocol is formally revised.
